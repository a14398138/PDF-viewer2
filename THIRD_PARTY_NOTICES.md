# Paper Renamer

Source: https://github.com/a14398138/paper-renamer
Revision: a94c8165001885797236fcb3f2cf336710d44d36 (v1.0.2)

The DOI extractor, name formatter, candidate models, PDF scanner, Crossref client
and their original unit tests are derived from Paper Renamer.
The integration changes the package, adds a Compose UI and lifecycle-aware state,
and synchronizes PDF Viewer history after filesystem renames.
